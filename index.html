Company History

Est 1996

As one of the first full-scale modular cabinet manufacturers in the Philippines, ModulPlus System Corporation (“Modulplus”) has supplied the global kitchen, closet, bathroom, entertainment, and office furniture industry since 1996. Modulplus’ other products include counter tops, bathroom fixtures, engineered flooring, and other casework.

  • 201-500 Employees

Our corporate culture emphasizes our clients first. We aim to consistently manufacture the highest quality products, to deliver, to install, and to turnover on time, and to offer fair pricing upfront with no “hidden” costs due to inferior quality or delays.

We source materials from a wide variety of suppliers, all of which have been tested for the highest standards of quality. We are also known to advise our clients in developing unique solutions for specific needs through value engineering, design consultation, strategic planning and product procurement.
